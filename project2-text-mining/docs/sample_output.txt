Purpose: shows how varied the vocabulary of the text is.
Formula: LDS = distinct words / total words x 100
Levels: 70+ Very high | 50-69 High | 30-49 Moderate | below 30 Low
Reading: a low score means the same words keep coming back; a high
score means most words appear only once. Short texts score
higher because they have less room to repeat.
